A QUICK TRAVEL TIP

It all fits.
Promise.

  1. Pick an essential.Drag it into the suitcase, or tap an item then a grid square.
  2. Find its good side.Use Rotate or press R, including on a piece already in the case. An overlapping turn stays visible: the clashing squares turn transparent red, and the turn only locks in once you slide them clear. Locked pockets are sewn into the case and cannot move. Pack around them and follow your passenger’s requests. The dotted guide shows the space each item needs.
  3. Get ready for takeoff.Pack every item, then zip it up for a passport stamp. A hint first outlines a spot; tap again to place the item.

★ Pack everything. ★★ Finish without hints. ★★★ Use no hints and stay within the displayed move target. Placing, moving, unpacking and undoing each cost one move. Turning a packed piece costs one move when that angle fits. If it overlaps, the red squares are not a move until you slide them clear. Turning in the tray is free. Saved on this browser.

Keyboard: Tab to an item and press Enter. On the suitcase, arrow keys move; Enter places; R rotates. Select a packed item to move or unpack it.
